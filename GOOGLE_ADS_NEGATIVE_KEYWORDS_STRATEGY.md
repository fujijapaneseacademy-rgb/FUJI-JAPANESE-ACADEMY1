# Fuji Japanese Academy: Google Ads Negative Keywords & Search Intent Dominance Strategy

> **Target Audience:** High-intent paying students, software engineers, IT executives, corporate teams, and JLPT exam candidates aiming for career relocation to Japan.  
> **Objective:** Eliminate 100% of wasted ad spend from anime streamers, manga pirates, Google Translate users, and casual hobbyists. Maximize Quality Score, CTR, and Conversion Rate (ROAS).

---

## 1. The Critical Problem: Search Query Pollution in Japanese Education

Without strict negative keyword lists, 65% to 80% of Google Ads budget in the Japanese language niche is drained by users searching for:
- Free anime streaming episodes and torrent downloads
- Manga scanlations and webtoon reading sites
- Quick copy-paste translations for tattoos, discord bios, or lyrics
- "How do you say X in Japanese" slang or anime dialogue
- Free PDF downloads of Minna no Nihongo or JLPT question papers

By enforcing this master negative keyword architecture, every rupee spent is directed exclusively to users actively seeking **coaching institutes, JLPT certification courses, native-speaker classes, or enterprise training**.
